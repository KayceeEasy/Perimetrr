Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

What Perimetrr stores in your browser and whether optional tracking is used.

More than cookies

Perimetrr uses browser local storage, session storage and cached assets. Rules about cookies can also apply to these technologies. This release does not include advertising pixels, behavioral marketing analytics or social/video embeds.

Storage used by the app

  • Authentication: account session information used to keep administrators signed in. Sign out on shared devices.
  • Pairing: a persistent browser credential and linked workspace/staff hints used to identify an authorized browser.
  • Offline operation: queued attendance attempts, including time and submitted location, and cached application assets.
  • Requested preferences: theme, language and relevant workspace/schedule display preferences.

These items generally remain until replaced, cleared, signed out or removed by the browser; session storage normally ends with the browsing session. Clearing site data can remove pairing and require an approved transfer. It does not automatically delete records already held by your employer.

Optional tracking

Automatic browser diagnostic-event collection has been disabled for this beta. There is no optional tracking category to accept. A decorative “Accept all” banner is therefore not provided. This does not mean all storage is exempt in every country: persistent authentication and preference storage must be assessed for the actual deployment and purpose. If optional analytics, advertising or embeds are added, they must remain off until any required consent is given, with an equally easy way to refuse and withdraw.

External resources

Supabase, the hosting provider, Google Fonts, jsDelivr and unpkg can receive technical network information when the app requests their services. No-cookie does not mean no personal-data processing. See the Privacy Policy.

Your controls

Use your browser’s site-data and permission settings to inspect or remove storage and revoke location permission. Sign out before leaving a shared device. No optional cookie consent is bundled with acceptance of the terms. Questions: support@perimetrr.com.
