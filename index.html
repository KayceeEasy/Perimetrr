      Privacy & Location Policy

      The Perimetrr Workspace Platform uses location permission for office Perimeter checks and a browser credential for staff pairing. These checks support attendance verification; they do not prove a person’s identity or prevent GPS spoofing.

      Location & Perimeter Checks

      With your permission, the open app checks location to display your distance from the office Perimeter. When you submit attendance, coordinates are sent to the server to verify that attempt. This is not a background route-tracking service. You can revoke location permission in your browser; location-dependent attendance checks will then be unavailable.

      🔔 Passive Perimeter Arrival Reminders

      With location permission, the open app can show whether you are inside the office Perimeter. When you record attendance, your coordinates are sent to the server for a distance check. Background arrival alerts are not guaranteed by mobile browsers.

      Linked Browser Credential

      A browser credential is checked against the staff profile stored in Supabase. It is not a hardware identifier or proof of the employee’s identity. Clearing browser storage or using a different browser requires linking or an approved transfer.

      🏢 Multi-Tenant Data Isolation & Employee Rights

      Attendance logs (timestamp, verification method, Perimeter status and submitted coordinates) are processed using the service providers described in our Privacy Policy. Authorized administrators can access workspace records. Ask your employer about access, correction and retention.
